Why this exists

ChillMate is made by one person in the Netherlands. It is free, it has no ads, and it is open source. There is no company behind it and no plan to build one.

The thing it is trying to fix #

Plenty of apps hold the most sensitive things about you: how you slept, what you took, who you were with, what you are worried about. Almost all of them keep a copy on a server, and almost all of them say so, in a paragraph nobody reads.

ChillMate is the version of that app where the copy does not exist. Not encrypted-at-rest on someone's cloud, not anonymised, not retained for ninety days. There is no server, so there is nothing to leak, subpoena, sell, or change its mind about later.

That constraint decides most of the design. It is why there is no account. It is why sync means your iCloud, not mine. And it is why the app has to work properly offline, in a taxi, at four in the morning.

The first two seconds #

This plays once, the very first time the app opens, and then never again. It is a small thing to have spent time on. It is also the whole argument in one gesture. Nothing is asked of you. Nothing is signed up for. The mark draws itself, then gets out of the way.

The rules it is built to #

  • No server, no account. If a feature would need one, the feature is wrong, not the rule.
  • Nothing is ever sold. Not now, not at scale, not to a buyer later.
  • Free means free. The tip unlocks nothing. Every feature is available to everyone.
  • It never decides for you. ChillMate tells you what is known and hands the decision back.
  • Help before features. Crisis numbers, breathing and grounding work with no data, no signal, and no setup.
  • Open, so it can be checked. The claim on the front page is only worth something if you can read the code behind it.

How it is built #

Swift and SwiftUI, SwiftData for storage, HealthKit, WidgetKit, App Intents and a native watchOS app. Everything on-device, including the written summaries, which use Apple's on-device Foundation Models rather than a hosted one.

Version 4.2.1 (build 422). It runs on iOS 26 and later, with an Apple Watch app, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, complications, Siri shortcuts and Spotlight.

Every push builds and runs the test suite on GitHub Actions. You can see the runs, passing or not, on the CI workflow. There is deliberately no build badge on this page. A badge is an image loaded from someone else's server, so it would hand your IP address to a stranger. On a site built around not doing that, it would be an odd thing to add.

See the changelog for what has landed, or the repository for everything else.
