Who it is for

Made for the nights this is actually about

Chems, hookups, or both. ChillMate has no opinion about that, and it will never ask you to justify a night.

  • A risk checker that knows what you already take, including your own prescription.
  • A check-in timer, so somebody knows you got home without you having to text them.
  • A PEP window that starts counting the moment it might matter.
  • Testing reminders that never say why on your lock screen.

The app calls a night a Chill. Yours, whatever it was.

Private by design

Your phone, and nowhere else

Privacy here is not a setting. It is the shape of the app.

  • Lock it behind Face ID or a PIN, with everything encrypted on the device.4
  • Backups are optional and encrypted, and they go to your own iCloud Drive.5
  • Notifications can stay deliberately vague on the lock screen.6

Where your data goes

Do not take my word for it

ChillMate is open source. Every line that touches your data is public, which turns the claim above into something you can check.

There is not a single network call in the app's own code. No analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no URLSession anywhere. The only things that leave your phone are the ones you tap.1
