These two files are the latin and latin-ext subsets of the Inter variable font,
as served by Google Fonts, saved here so the site fetches them from its own
origin. Nothing on this site is loaded from a third party, and a webfont
request would otherwise hand every reader's IP address to whoever served it.

They are only ever downloaded by browsers that cannot resolve -apple-system,
which is to say: not by anyone on an Apple device, who already has SF Pro.
