Every rating here is checked against TripSit's published drug combination chart, and each row says how it compares. Where ChillMate rates something higher, it says so. A missing entry means nobody has rated that pair, not that nothing happens.

The medication groups it recognises

  • Affects serotonin ssri, snri, tramadol, lithium, linezolid, mirtazapine
  • MAOI maoi, phenelzine, tranylcypromine, moclobemide, selegiline
  • Sedative benzodiazepine, benzo, diazepam, alprazolam, lorazepam, oxazepam
  • Opioid opioid, opiate, oxycodone, morphine, fentanyl, codeine
  • Nitrate-like nitrate, nitroglycerin, glyceryl trinitrate, isosorbide, mononitrate, dinitrate
  • Alpha blocker alpha blocker, tamsulosin, doxazosin, alfuzosin, prazosin, terazosin
  • Stimulant medication methylphenidate, ritalin, concerta, dexamfetamine, dexamphetamine, lisdexamfetamine
  • Ritonavir/cobicistat ritonavir, cobicistat
