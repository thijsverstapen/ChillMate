# ChillMate 4.1.0 — App Store "What's New"

## English (en-US / en-GB)

ChillMate 4.1.0 — calmer, smarter, and there exactly when you need it.

A HOME THAT KNOWS THE MOMENT
• Redesigned home: your tools are now grouped into four clear moments — Before you go, While you're out, Aftercare & health, and Your patterns — instead of a wall of buttons.
• It arranges itself. When a dose timer is running, or it's the morning after, the tool you need rises to the top.
• "Get help now" is always one tap away, pinned at the top, so breathing, grounding, and emergency calls are never something you have to hunt for.

SIMPLER TO MOVE AROUND
• Three calm tabs — Home, History, and More. Your calendar and journal now live together under History.

SAFETY WHEN IT MATTERS MOST
• New optional weekend night check-ins: on Friday and Saturday nights, a discreet, gentle "you okay?" with one-tap "I'm safe" or "Get help" — for the hours when a session is most likely.

NOTIFICATIONS ON YOUR TERMS
• Choose exactly which reminders you want right in setup: night safety, session check-ins, weekly summary, and STI reminders — all optional, all clearly explained.

ALSO
• A gentler onboarding that introduces the four moments, consistent tool cards across every screen, and many small refinements.
