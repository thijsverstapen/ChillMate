# ChillMate 4.3.0: App Store "What's New"

## English (en-US / en-GB)

ChillMate 4.3.0, tidier, faster to reach, and finally speaking your language everywhere.

SETTINGS THAT MAKE SENSE
• Settings is now five clear groups instead of eleven long cards.
• Auto-lock no longer squeezes its own description. Title, explanation, and choice each get their own line.
• iCloud backup finally tells you when it last ran, and Back up now and Restore are the same size.

YOUR PROFILE ON ONE PAGE
• Your profile used to be four cards that only said how many items each held. Everything now sits on one page: identity, body, health, and medication, all at a glance.
• Fixed: on Dutch, German, French, and Spanish devices those profile sections came up empty. They show your details properly now.

ASK WITHOUT OPENING THE APP
• New Siri actions that actually do something: check how two substances combine and hear the verdict read back, start a dose timer, or log a substance, all without unlocking.
• New Control Centre buttons: open panic support straight from the Lock Screen, or log water without opening anything.
• Because these take real inputs, you can build them into Shortcuts automations, like starting a timer when you arrive somewhere.

A RISK CHECKER THAT ANSWERS MORE OFTEN
• The combination table went from 30 rated pairs to all 66. Alcohol with poppers, MDMA with poppers, GHB with MDMA, cocaine with ketamine and cannabis with almost everything used to return "nothing on file". Now they return real guidance.
• Three new checks alongside the existing ones: Breathing, Heart strain, and Blood pressure. Breathing folds in prescribed sedatives and opioids, because those stack with everything else.

A LIVE ACTIVITY WORTH GLANCING AT
• The dose timer on your Lock Screen now shows a progress bar that fills as the session runs, plus the time it ends.
• Log water without unlocking, from the Live Activity or the Dynamic Island.

NOTIFICATIONS THAT KNOW WHAT MATTERS
• Urgent reminders, like a PEP window or a redose nudge, are now marked Time Sensitive, so they can reach you through a Focus. Ambient ones like daily affirmations stay quiet in the background. Until now every alert arrived at the same volume.

YOUR DATA, YOUR RULES
• Data retention now offers eight windows, from one month to five years, instead of five.
• New: let ChillMate apply your retention window automatically, once a day, so you never have to remember.

APPLE HEALTH AND APPLE WATCH
• ChillMate can now read resting heart rate and breathing rate as recovery signals, and write your breathing sessions as mindful minutes.
• New on the watch: "I'm home safe". It stops tonight's safety check-ins, because the question they were asking has been answered.

SPEAKING YOUR LANGUAGE
• Dozens of texts stayed in English no matter which language you picked. The breathing prompts in panic support, the auto-lock and retention choices, the STI testing coverage table, the setup labels, and the whole Live Activity all translate now.

EASIER TO USE FOR EVERYONE
• VoiceOver can jump between headings on every page.
• The breathing guide announces each phase out loud, and grounding steps say whether they are ticked.
• Every settings row now explains where it leads.

ALSO
• Panic support starts on "Breathe in" instead of "Hold gently", gives you a longer exhale, and stops the timer when you leave the screen.
• Ready for iOS 27: navigation bars tuck away as you scroll while Save and Start stay put.
