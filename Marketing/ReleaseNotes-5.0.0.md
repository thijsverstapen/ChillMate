# ChillMate 5.0.0: App Store "What's New"

Paste each section into its locale in App Store Connect. Every one is under the
4,000 character limit, which the previous draft was not.

The full, detailed list of what changed is on the site at
`/changelog/` and is generated from `tools/build_site.py`.

## English (en-US / en-GB)

ChillMate 5.0.0

TWO SUBSTANCES IT COULD NOT NAME
• Benzodiazepines and meth can now be logged and checked. That is 25 new rated combinations.
• Every rating is now compared against TripSit's published chart, and each warning tells you how it compares.
• That comparison found four ChillMate was rating too low. They are at the highest severity now.

THE DAY AFTER
• Dose info now shows how long after-effects last, not just how long the effects do. MDMA runs 12 to 48 hours past the end.
• Timings are per route. A cannabis edible takes 20 to 60 minutes to arrive, not the under-ten of smoking.
• A running timer shows which phase you are in, and keeps showing it after the timer ends.

WITHOUT UNLOCKING
• A Lock Screen widget for a running dose.
• A check-in for the way home. Say roughly how long you will be; one tap closes it. No destination is shown, and nothing is sent to anyone.
• Ask Siri for panic support.

STRAIGHT IN
• Setup can be skipped from the first screen. Nothing in the app is locked behind it.
• The opening animation can be tapped away, and is skipped if you have asked your phone for less motion.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
• A lot was appearing in English whatever you chose: drug information, PrEP reminders, first-run messages, and the default text you send a trusted contact. All translated now.

ALSO
• Bigger text sizes no longer cut labels in half.
• The calendar reads out properly and starts on your region's first day of the week.
• Emergency signs are specific to each substance, with a step-by-step for the minutes before an ambulance arrives.
• Fixed: a weekly digest that told everyone they had a zero streak, a widget that said "1 days", and a watch setting connected to nothing.

## Français (fr-FR)

ChillMate 5.0.0

SANS DÉVERROUILLER

• La configuration peut être passée dès le premier écran. Rien dans l'app n'est verrouillé derrière.

DANS TA LANGUE
